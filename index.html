Biography

Tyler The Creator is a Grammy-winning rapper, producer, songwriter and creative director. Emerging as the founder of Odd Future, he reshaped alternative hip-hop with bold production and visionary storytelling.

He won Grammy Awards for IGOR and Call Me If You Get Lost. His influential albums include Goblin, Wolf, Cherry Bomb, Flower Boy, IGOR, and Call Me If You Get Lost.

Iconic singles include Yonkers, See You Again, EARFQUAKE, and WUSYANAME.

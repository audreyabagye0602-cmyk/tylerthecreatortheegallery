About

Tyler, The Creator is a Grammy Award-winning rapper, producer, and creative visionary. He has won multiple Grammy Awards including Best Rap Album for IGOR (2020) and Call Me If You Get Lost (2022). His discography includes influential albums such as Bastard, Goblin, Wolf, Cherry Bomb, Flower Boy, IGOR, and Call Me If You Get Lost. Known for reinventing his sound, Tyler blends jazz, soul, rap, and experimental production. He is also the founder of Golf Wang and Golf le Fleur. Tyler is the goat of music :)
